01. About

Hi! I'm Henderson, a game developer with a degree in Computer Science. Since I was a kid, I've been passionate about games, and that passion sparked my curiosity to understand how a game is made, how its mechanics work, and what makes an idea truly work out.

I've worked with Unity since 2020, developing 2D and 3D games with C# — and increasingly everything around games: backend, publishing and optimization. I keep studying through courses, game jams and my own projects.

Currently, I'm involved in indie projects and open to new opportunities to keep working in game development. Here you can find some of the projects I've worked on and a bit of what I've been doing along the way.

I'm always open to interesting projects, collaborations, and conversations with people who enjoy making games as much as I do. If you'd like to get in touch, feel free to reach out.
